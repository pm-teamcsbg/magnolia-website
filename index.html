Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a Florida beach trip: where should we stay?

Most Florida beach trips come down to a choice between the Atlantic side and the Gulf Coast. The Gulf offers calmer, warmer water, sugar-white sand, and nightly sunsets over the water. On the Gulf, low-rise beach towns like Indian Rocks Beach, between Clearwater and St. Petersburg, deliver the beach without the crowds, and a waterfront home base like Magnolia By The Sea puts a private pool, dock, and golf cart at the center of the trip.

Why is Indian Rocks Beach the best beach town on Florida's Gulf Coast?

Indian Rocks Beach packs miles of white Gulf sand into a laid-back, golf-cart-friendly town with no high-rises and famous Gulf sunsets. You get a quiet, residential beach feel while staying central to everything: Clearwater is 10 minutes away, John's Pass 15, and downtown St. Petersburg and Tampa International Airport about 30. Big-beach access at a small-town pace.

How does Indian Rocks Beach compare to Clearwater Beach and Madeira Beach?

Indian Rocks Beach offers the same white Gulf sand as its neighbors without the high-rise hotels and spring-break crowds of Clearwater Beach. It's a quieter, golf-cart-friendly residential town, better suited to families and groups who want space. Clearwater's attractions are 10 minutes north and John's Pass at Madeira Beach is 15 minutes south, so you can visit both and come home to calm.

When is the best time to visit Indian Rocks Beach?

Indian Rocks Beach is a year-round destination. March through August are the busiest months, while October and November bring the best weather of the year. The holidays are special here too: Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's often book far in advance. Winters stay mild, and the home's heated saltwater pool keeps swimming on the agenda.

Where is Magnolia By The Sea located?

Very. Indian Rocks Beach is a low-key, golf-cart-friendly beach town with calm Gulf water, no high-rise crowds, and casual restaurants. The house adds a bunk room kids love, a heated pool, kayaks, and a pack and play and high chair on request. Clearwater Marine Aquarium is 7 miles away.
